About

Hi, I'm Jeff! I'm an undergraduate at Seoul National University, studying Electrical and Computer Engineering and Mathematical Sciences.

I'm interested in building computer systems that are both fast and resource-efficient through hardware–software co-design. Most of my work so far has been on making Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) practical — from algorithms and libraries, to GPU kernels, to running LLM inference on encrypted data.

Along the way, I've been fortunate to work with Prof. Wooseok Choi and Prof. Jung Hee Cheon at SNU, the team at CryptoLab, and Prof. Michael Orshansky at UT Austin, where I spent Spring 2026 as an exchange student.

Research Interests

  • Computer Architecture & SystemsArchitecture- and system-level optimization for programmable, scalable workloads.
  • HW/SW Co-Design for Emerging WorkloadsDesigning algorithms, software, and hardware together around real hardware limits.
  • Secure ComputationCurrently focused on accelerating FHE on GPUs and scaling encrypted LLM inference.
